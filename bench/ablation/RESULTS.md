## Analysis

### What the grid shows

The headline win is 890 → 415, a cut of −475 total IDS. Stacked table B decomposes it: baseline 890 → +gate_m 718 → +vel 718 → +birth 0.5 618 → +iou 619 → +birth 0.7 415. The win is carried by the gate tightening (−172), the birth-score raise to 0.5 (−100), and the further birth-score raise to 0.7 (−204); vel contributes 0 in-stack and iou contributes +1 in-stack. The three in-stack components (−172, −100, −204) sum to −476, within 1 IDS of the observed −475. Read alone off baseline, the marginal table tells a noisier per-knob story: gate only −172, vel only +26, iou only −12, birth 0.5 only −117, birth 0.7 only −334.

### Finding 001 decomposition

Finding 001 was measured as one bundle of gate_m + vel_cost_weight + min_birth_score 0.5 (post001, 618). Table B shows the bundle decomposes into the gate (−172) and the birth-score raise (−100), with the velocity cost contributing 0 in-stack. The interaction notes show the interesting flip: vel alone off baseline is harmful (+26), yet in-stack it is neutral (0). The gate tightness and the birth-score raise carry the win; the velocity cost is redundant once the gate is already tight.

### 002/IoU is a reproduced null

The iou knob does not contribute to the stacked win. In-stack (table B) it is +1, within the ±20 landmark noise tolerance; marginal (alone off baseline) it is −12. iou does not survive into the stack — this is a reproduced null, reported explicitly rather than suppressed. (If the tables had shown iou suddenly winning in the stack, that would be a new finding to report; they do not.)

### Interactions

From the interaction notes table, marginal vs in-stack: birth 0.7's marginal (−334) differs from its in-stack (−204) by −130, vel by +26, iou by −13, birth 0.5 by −17. The knobs are not additive: each knob's marginal effect shrinks once motion-consistent matching (the tightened gate) is already on, most sharply for birth 0.7, whose standalone effect is nearly halved in-stack.
